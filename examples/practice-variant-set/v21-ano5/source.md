## Variant Impact Data
- Exon 4 is present in the MANE Select transcript
- Exon 4 has variable expression across tissues but is highly expressed in skeletal muscle
- SpliceAI prediction is just above >0.20 threshold suggested for predicted impact to splicing
- Loss of exon 4 acceptor would be expended to result in skipping of exon 4
- Exon 4 is inframe and 13 AAs in length p.Pro47_Met60del
- Loss of this exon would remove ~1.5% of the protein (13/913)
- Exon 4 encodes part of the cytoplasmic region
- There are only 2 P/LP variants in this exon (ClinVar screenshot at right)
- One is a nonsense variant which would not be informative for if this exon codes a critical region
- However the other is a missense variant with supporting clinical data
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/197402/
- As there is evidence of P/LP missense variants in this exon, this could be considered under:
- "Removes/alters >10% of protein OR
- Removes/alters a functional domain with some evidence in the Molecular Mechanism"
