## Variant Impact Data
- Variant is predicted to lead to inframe skipping of exon 33 (c.4910_5005)
- Exon 33 is part of the HEAT REPEAT domain (exons 2 to 38), which the ATM VCEP has a critical region
- This skipping of this inframe exon would remove only a portion of the larger critical domain, loss of this exon can be considered as "Removes/alters a portion of a critical functional domain that has been experimentally implicated in the Molecular Mechanism"
- Another variant at this same position (c.5005+1G>T) has been classified as LP by the ATM VCEP (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/variation/371636/)
- The +1G>T change has an equal SpliceAI prediction as the +1G>A variant and has the same predicted outcome (inframe skipping of this exon)
