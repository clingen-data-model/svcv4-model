## Variant Impact Data
- NM_000330.4:c.53-713_78+266del is a deletion that starts in intron 1, includes all of exon 2, and ends in intron 2
- Exon 2 is out of frame and would be expected to result in p.(Ala18GlyfsTer2)
- Restricting to just VCEP variants, there are 2 Pathogenic and 1 Likely pathogenic LoF variant in this exon
- (Since from VCEP okay to assume clinical data were present for these variants)
- This deletion does remove the acceptor and donor for exon 2 which would also result in exon skipping
- Completion of SPL workflow is not required for this variant type
