## Variant Impact Data
- No predicted impact on splicing
- This duplication variant resulting in a frameshift change occurs in exon 9 of 9 in RUNX1.
- This frameshit if not predicted to undergo NMD as the NMD boundary for this gene is c.917 (VBC is c.1412_1413dup)
- However, this frameshift (which starts at Leu472) does alter aa 472-480, which includes the VWRPY motif
- Altering this domain can be considered in "Removes/alters a portion of a critical functional domain that has been experimentally implicated in the Molecular Mechanism"
- Additionally, this frameshift results in an elongation; native stop is at position 481
- New stop codon would be >100 codons past native stop codon
- This variant can be assessed in the frameshift workflow under "NMD is not predicted" and "Non–stop Decay is not Predicted" - and the path with the most points awarded
- There is also a del at this position (NM_001754.5(RUNX1):c.1412_1413del (p.Arg471fs) classified as LP however no clinical evidence was applied for this LP variant
