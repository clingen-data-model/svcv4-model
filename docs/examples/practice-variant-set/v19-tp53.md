## The classification, four ways

=== "Prose"

    TP53 c.641A>T (p.His214Leu) is assessed for whether it **is causal for** Li-Fraumeni syndrome (`MONDO:0018875`), under AD inheritance, under the baseline SVCv4 specification. 3 lines of evidence — population allele frequency, functional assay, single-amino-acid change — compose to an illustrative **likely pathogenic**.

=== "Narrative"

    The variant being classified (VBC) is TP53 c.641A>T (p.His214Leu); the disease/condition (MDE) is Li-Fraumeni syndrome. The curator captured:

    - **Population allele frequency (POP_FRQ)** (`POP_FRQ_+0`, score 0.0) — Population frequency (illustrative).
    - **Functional assay (FNC)** (`FNC_+4`, score 4.0) — Functional assays (PFD, illustrative).
    - **Single-amino-acid change (MIS)** (`MIS_+1`, score 1.0) — Same-residue (PM5-type) evidence (PFD, illustrative).

    Each became an Evidence Line; their scores compose to a Statement final score of 5.0 → *likely pathogenic*.

=== "Semi-structured"

    ```text
    Statement
      proposition:
        subjectVariant (VBC): TP53 c.641A>T (p.His214Leu)
        predicate:            is_causal_for
        objectCondition (MDE): MONDO:0018875 (Li-Fraumeni syndrome)
        qualifiers:           moi=AD; note=Loss of function is an established disease mechanism.
      method:        svcv4:baseline
      evidence_lines:
        - POP_FRQ_+0   score  0.0
        - FNC_+4       score  4.0
        - MIS_+1       score  1.0
      final_score:          5.0
      score_classification: likely_pathogenic
    ```
