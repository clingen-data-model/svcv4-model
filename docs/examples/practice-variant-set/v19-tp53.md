## The classification, four ways

=== "Prose"

    TP53 c.641A>T (p.His214Leu) is assessed for whether it **is causal for** Li-Fraumeni syndrome (`MONDO:0018875`), under AD inheritance, under the baseline SVCv4 specification. 3 lines of evidence — population allele frequency, functional assay, single-amino-acid change — compose to an illustrative **likely pathogenic**.

=== "Narrative"

    The variant being classified (VBC) is TP53 c.641A>T (p.His214Leu); the disease/condition (MDE) is Li-Fraumeni syndrome. The curator captured:

    - **Population allele frequency (POP_FRQ)** (`POP_FRQ`, score 0.0) — Population frequency (illustrative).
    - **Functional assay (FNC)** (`FNC`, score 4.0) — Functional assays (PFD, illustrative).
      - `FNC_ASY` (score 4.0) · *provisional* — Functional assay result.
    - **Single-amino-acid change (MIS)** (`MIS`, score 1.0) — Same-residue (PM5-type) evidence (PFD, illustrative).
      - `MIS_INF` (score 1.0) — Same-residue comparator evidence.
        - `INF_SAMEAA` (score 1.0) · *provisional* — Same-amino-acid change comparator (PS1-type; illustrative).

    Each became an Evidence Line; their scores compose to a Statement final score of 5.0 → *likely pathogenic*.
