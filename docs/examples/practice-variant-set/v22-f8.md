## The classification, four ways

=== "Prose"

    F8 c.1420G>C (p.Gly474Arg) is assessed for whether it **is causal for** hemophilia A (`MONDO:0010602`), under XLR inheritance, under the baseline SVCv4 specification. 2 lines of evidence — population allele frequency, single-amino-acid change — compose to an illustrative **likely pathogenic**.

=== "Narrative"

    The variant being classified (VBC) is F8 c.1420G>C (p.Gly474Arg); the disease/condition (MDE) is hemophilia A. The curator captured:

    - **Population allele frequency (POP_FRQ)** (`POP_FRQ_+0`, score 0.0) — Population frequency (illustrative).
    - **Single-amino-acid change (MIS)** (`MIS_+5`, score 5.0) — Predictive missense + same-AA (PS1) + same-codon (PM5) evidence (PFD, illustrative).

    Each became an Evidence Line; their scores compose to a Statement final score of 5.0 → *likely pathogenic*.

=== "Semi-structured"

