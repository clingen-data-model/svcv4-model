## The classification, four ways

=== "Prose"

    F8 c.1420G>C (p.Gly474Arg) is assessed for whether it **is causal for** hemophilia A (`MONDO:0010602`), under XLR inheritance, under the baseline SVCv4 specification. 2 lines of evidence — population allele frequency, single-amino-acid change — compose to an illustrative **likely pathogenic**.

=== "Narrative"

    The variant being classified (VBC) is F8 c.1420G>C (p.Gly474Arg); the disease/condition (MDE) is hemophilia A. The curator captured:

    - **Population allele frequency (POP_FRQ)** (`POP_FRQ`, score 0.0) — Population frequency (illustrative).
    - **Single-amino-acid change (MIS)** (`MIS`, score 5.0) — Predictive missense + same-AA (PS1) + same-codon (PM5) evidence (PFD, illustrative).
      - `MIS_PRD` (score 4.0) — In-silico predictive missense assessment.
        - `MIS_PRD_INIT_REVEL` (score 4.0) · *provisional* — Strong in-silico missense support (REVEL 0.972).
      - `MIS_INF` (score 1.0) — Same-residue comparator evidence.
        - `INF_SAMEAA` (score 1.0) · *provisional* — Same-amino-acid change comparator (PS1-type; illustrative).
        - `INF_DISTAA` (score 0.0) · *provisional* — Same-codon, different-amino-acid comparators (PM5-type; illustrative).

    Each became an Evidence Line; their scores compose to a Statement final score of 5.0 → *likely pathogenic*.
